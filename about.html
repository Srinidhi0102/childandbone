Our Story

Child & Bone Medical Center was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide comprehensive healthcare that addresses the unique needs of both children and adults requiring orthopedic care. Our practice brings together the expertise of pediatric medicine and orthopedic surgery under one roof.

We believe that healthcare should be accessible, compassionate, and family-centered. Our team of board-certified specialists works together to ensure that every patient receives personalized care tailored to their specific needs.

Dr. Purvi Kamath

Consultant Pediatrician and Neonatologist (Child Specialist)

MBBS; MD Pediatrics (Gold Medal, Sion Hospital, Mumbai); DNB; Lactation Counsellor (BPNI)

About Me

Hello! I am Dr. Purvi Kamath, a pediatrician dedicated to caring for children’s health and well-being. My special interests include child development, nutrition, breastfeeding support, and immunisation. I have extensive experience in treating various childhood illnesses and in newborn care.

I completed my MBBS from Dr. VM Government Medical College, Solapur, and went on to pursue MD Pediatrics at Sion Hospital, Mumbai, where I graduated with a gold medal and ranked 3rd in Maharashtra. I also hold a DNB in Pediatrics and am a certified lactation counsellor with BPNI Maharashtra.

Over the years, I have gained valuable experience through senior residency at both Sion Hospital and Rajiv Gandhi Medical College & Hospital, Kalwa. I have also worked at Bethany Hospital, Thane, and continue to provide pediatric care at various hospitals and clinics. Alongside clinical practice, I have presented my work at national forums and published two research papers in international journals.

My goal is to offer holistic, compassionate, and evidence-based care for every child, while supporting parents in their journey through growth, development, and preventive health.
