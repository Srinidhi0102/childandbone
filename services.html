Our Medical Services

Comprehensive healthcare services for children and orthopedic care for all ages.

Pediatric Services

Comprehensive healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents from birth through young adulthood.

Well-Child Visits

Regular checkups to monitor your child's growth, development, and overall health.

  • Growth and development monitoring
  • Physical examinations
  • Health screenings
  • Parent education and guidance

Vaccinations

Complete immunization services following CDC guidelines to protect your child's health.

  • Routine childhood vaccines
  • Flu vaccinations
  • Travel immunizations
  • Vaccine counseling

Developmental Assessments

Early detection and intervention for developmental delays and learning differences.

  • Developmental screening
  • Autism spectrum evaluations
  • Learning disability assessments
  • Behavioral consultations

Acute Illness Care

Prompt treatment for common childhood illnesses and infections.

  • Fever and infection treatment
  • Respiratory illness care
  • Ear, nose, and throat issues
  • Skin condition treatment

Orthopedic Services

Advanced treatment for bone, joint, and muscle conditions using the latest surgical and non-surgical techniques.

Joint Replacement

Advanced joint replacement surgery for hips, knees, and shoulders to restore mobility and reduce pain.

  • Hip replacement surgery
  • Knee replacement surgery
  • Shoulder replacement
  • Revision surgery

Sports Medicine

Specialized care for athletes and active individuals with sports-related injuries.

  • ACL reconstruction
  • Meniscus repair
  • Rotator cuff surgery
  • Sports injury rehabilitation

Fracture Care

Comprehensive treatment for broken bones, from simple fractures to complex trauma cases.

  • Emergency fracture treatment
  • Complex fracture surgery
  • Pediatric fracture care
  • Non-surgical fracture management

Arthroscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive procedures for joint problems with faster recovery times.

  • Knee arthroscopy
  • Shoulder arthroscopy
  • Ankle arthroscopy
  • Wrist and elbow procedures

Additional Services

Emergency Care

Diagnostic Imaging

Physical Therapy

Comprehensive rehabilitation services to restore function and mobility.

Consultations

Second opinions and specialized consultations for complex cases.
